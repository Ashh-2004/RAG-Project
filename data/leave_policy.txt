Company Leave Policy Guidelines

1. Annual Leave Entitlement:
   - Full-time employees are entitled to 15 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
   - Leave accrues at a rate of 1.25 days per month worked.
   - A maximum of 5 unused annual leave days can be carried forward to the following calendar year.

2. Sick Leave:
   - Employees are provided with 7 days of paid sick leave per year.
   - Sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days requires a valid medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner.

3. Casual Leave:
   - Employees receive 5 days of casual leave per year for urgent personal matters.
   - Casual leave cannot be combined with annual leave without prior managerial approval.

4. Leave Application Process:
   - All leave requests must be submitted at least 3 business days in advance via the HR Portal or Employee AI Assistant.
   - Emergency leave requests should be communicated directly to the reporting manager and HR within 24 hours of absence.
   - Leave approvals are subject to team bandwidth and project deliverables.
