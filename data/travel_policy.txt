Corporate Travel & Expense Policy

1. Travel Approval & Bookings:
   - All business travel must be pre-approved by the Department Head at least 7 days prior to domestic travel and 14 days for international travel.
   - All flights and hotel accommodations must be booked through the corporate travel portal.

2. Daily Per Diem Allowances:
   - Domestic Travel: $50 per day covering meals and incidental expenses.
   - International Travel: $100 per day covering meals and incidental expenses.
   - Itemized receipts are mandatory for any individual expense exceeding $25.

3. Accommodation Tiers:
   - Employees are eligible for standard business hotel accommodation (up to 4-star category or maximum $150/night for domestic, $250/night for international).

4. Expense Reimbursement Timeline:
   - Expense reports along with scanned receipts must be submitted within 7 business days following the end of the trip.
   - Reimbursements will be processed in the upcoming payroll cycle.
