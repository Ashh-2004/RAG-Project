Work From Home (WFH) & Hybrid Work Policy

1. Work From Home Allowance:
   - Eligible full-time employees can work remotely for up to 2 days per week (maximum 8 days per month).
   - Core operational days (Tuesday and Thursday) require mandatory in-office presence unless prior manager exemption is granted.

2. Core Working Hours & Availability:
   - Remote employees must maintain core working hours between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM local time.
   - Employees are expected to be reachable via Slack/Teams and email during core working hours.

3. Equipment & Network Requirements:
   - Employees working remotely must ensure a high-speed broadband internet connection (minimum 50 Mbps).
   - Use of company-issued laptop and VPN is mandatory for all remote work activities.
   - Working from public Wi-Fi without VPN encryption is strictly prohibited.

4. WFH Request Procedure:
   - WFH days must be requested at least 24 hours in advance via the HR system or AI assistant.
